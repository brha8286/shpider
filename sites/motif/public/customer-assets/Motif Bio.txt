The Motif Movement is a bass collective by the people, of the people, for the people. We formed to create a safe, high quality, and immersive space for all walks of life to listen to the bone shaking music that brought us all together. We take pride in our high fidelity Paraflex system, one of the best sounding rigs in West Texas. Regardless of if its a warehouse show, a full venue, or a soundcamp at a festival we aim to provide a top level experience that is garuanteed to have you shake a tail feather. Our roster is comprised of some of the best artists in the 806, and we are excited for the oppurtunity to share that art with all of you!   

Producer/DJs such as Big-A, Bass Slave, and Mask help push our scene forward with their original music, uniquely crafted sets, and thoughtful sonic innovations. 

Visual artists such as Lucid_Vision and Ne0n Croissant help bring the show to life with immersive and interactive visuals, stage design, lights, lasers...and a fair amount of pixie dust.
